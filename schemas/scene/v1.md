# Fixed scenario v1

Source is a self-contained OpenUSD stage (USDA or USDC), read through the pinned
official Python USD bindings. The committed USDA fixture is the reference
authoring example. Stage metadata must explicitly state `metersPerUnit`, use Y
up, and select `/Scenario` as default prim. Lengths are converted to integral
millimetres with at most 0.001 mm rounding error. Animated values, external
layers/references/payloads, inherits, specializes, authored relationships,
attribute connections, variants, instancing, asset dependencies, and additional
unsupported prims are rejected in this initial profile. They require a later
dependency/provenance contract.

Distances are integer millimetres. X points right, Y up, Z completes a
right-handed frame. Ground is at Y=0; the 20 m square interior is centred on
X=Z=0. Four enclosing walls extend outward from the interior boundary. Boxes use
centre and full size; spawns use capsule foot positions. Capsule height includes
both hemispheres.

The packed scene has eight u32 fields: interior width, interior depth, wall
height, wall thickness, capsule height, capsule diameter, box count, spawn
count. Then each box has a u32 identity, three i32 centre coordinates and three
u32 full sizes (28 bytes). Each spawn has a u32 identity and three i32 foot
coordinates (16 bytes). Identity order is strictly increasing within each
collection. V1 requires width/depth 20000, capsule height 1800, diameter 600,
two boxes, and four spawns: exactly 152 bytes.

Wall and box dimensions are positive and at most 20000 mm. Wall height must
accommodate the capsule. Boxes are ground-supported, fit inside the interior,
and have even full sizes so their face coordinates are integral. Box volumes
cannot overlap. Spawns have Y=0, keep the capsule inside the interior and clear
of boxes and other spawns. Strict separation is required; touching is not a
valid spawn. Source and runtime enforce the same geometric rules.

The root Xform `/Scenario` has custom attributes `blackflower:schema` (1),
`blackflower:scenario` (`mvp`), `blackflower:interior` (double2 width/depth),
and `blackflower:wall` (double2 height/thickness), in stage units. It has an
identity transform. Its `Blocks` Scope contains two Cube prims with positive
axis-aligned transforms and `blackflower:id` uint attributes. Its `Spawns` Scope
contains four translation-only Xforms with `blackflower:id` attributes;
translations identify capsule feet. Collections are sorted by identity.

`/Scenario/Participant` is an untransformed Y-axis Capsule. USD `height` is the
cylinder length, so total runtime height is `height + 2 * radius`. The reference
authors height 1.2 and radius 0.3 in metres. Its extent is provided for
authoring tools; the cooker derives dimensions from the schema attributes and
does not trust authored bounding boxes. No USD library is needed by the C++
runtime.

OpenUSD references:
[linear units](https://openusd.org/release/api/group___usd_geom_linear_units__group.html),
[Capsule dimensions](https://openusd.org/release/api/class_usd_geom_capsule.html).
