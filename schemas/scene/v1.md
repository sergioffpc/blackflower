# Scene v1

A scene aggregates collision shapes, lights and spawn points. Collections may be
empty. Enclosures and other structures are authored as collision shapes;
participant dimensions and gameplay rules are not part of the scene contract.

The loader checks record types and byte boundaries. It does not enforce physical
suitability, containment, separation, identity uniqueness or scenario-specific
quantities. Consumers determine whether content is suitable for their use.

## Coordinates and encoding

Distances are integer millimetres. X points right, Y up, and Z completes a
right-handed frame. Positions are relative to the scene origin. All integers are
little-endian; floating fields use little-endian IEEE 754 binary32.

The header contains three u32 counts, in order: collision shapes, lights and
spawn points. CollisionShape records follow the 12-byte header, followed by all
light records and then all spawn records. The payload contains exactly the
declared records, with no padding or trailing bytes. Integer widths define
representability; there are no policy count or size caps.

Each collision shape record starts with a u32 kind discriminator. Its body is
selected by that kind:

| Kind | CollisionShape   | Body fields in order                                               |
| ---- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | Axis-aligned box | u32 identity, three i32 centre coordinates, three u32 full extents |
| 2    | Sphere           | u32 identity, three i32 centre coordinates, u32 radius             |

A box record occupies 32 bytes including its discriminator; a sphere occupies 24
bytes. Unsupported discriminators are rejected.

Each light record starts with a u32 kind discriminator and a u32 identity. The
remaining body depends on the kind:

| Kind | Light            | Body fields after identity                                                                    |
| ---- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | PointLight       | Three i32 position coordinates, three binary32 linear RGB components, binary32 intensity      |
| 2    | DirectionalLight | Three binary32 direction components, three binary32 linear RGB components, binary32 intensity |

Both light records occupy 36 bytes including discriminator and identity.
Unsupported discriminators are rejected. Point lights emit omnidirectionally
from their position. Directional lights emit parallel rays along their
scene-space direction; consumers normalize this vector. Intensity is a
dimensionless multiplier of color. The loader preserves floating values without
imposing photometric or direction-normalization rules.

A spawn record contains a u32 identity and three i32 position coordinates,
occupying 16 bytes. The position is a placement origin; it implies no
participant shape, ground contact or gameplay role.

## OpenUSD authoring

The cooker reads a self-contained OpenUSD stage through the pinned Python
bindings. Metadata explicitly states `metersPerUnit`, uses Y up, and selects
`/Scenario` as default prim. Lengths must convert to representable integer
millimetres with at most 0.001 mm rounding error.

The root is an identity-transform Xform with `blackflower:schema` set to 1 and
three Scopes: `CollisionShapes`, `Lights` and `Spawns`. No scenario name,
enclosure or participant prim is required. Every collection member has a
`blackflower:id` uint attribute; the cooker sorts each collection by identity.

`CollisionShapes` accepts Cube and Sphere prims with positive axis-aligned
transforms. Cubes encode their transformed centre and full sizes. Spheres
require uniform scale and encode their transformed centre and radius. These
restrictions define the supported representation, not geometric validity or
playability.

`Lights` contains translation-only Xforms with `blackflower:lightType` token
(`point` or `directional`), `blackflower:color` float3 and
`blackflower:intensity` float attributes. Point lights use the translation as
their position. Directional lights use `blackflower:direction` float3 in scene
coordinates; translation has no effect. `Spawns` contains translation-only
Xforms whose translations define their positions.

Animated values, external layers, references, payloads, inherits, specializes,
authored relationships, attribute connections, variants, instancing, asset
dependencies and unsupported prim types are outside this authoring subset. No
USD library is needed by the C++ runtime.

Schema evolution follows the
[schema versioning policy](../../docs/development-process.md#schema-versioning).

## Consumer scenes

The cooker derives complete ServerScene, AgentScene and ClientScene values from
one source. Their collection encoding is shared; the pack magic selects the
concrete scene contract. Forbidden collections must have zero counts.

ServerScene and AgentScene contain identical geometry for collision, without
lights, visual assets or audio assets. Only ServerScene contains spawn points.
ClientScene contains the same collision geometry and the source lights, without
spawn points; visual meshes, materials and audio encoding remain unimplemented.
Each consumer loads its own complete scene without requiring another consumer's
pack.
